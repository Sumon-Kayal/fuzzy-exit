Fuzzy Exit word-combination lists
================================

exit_all_permutations.txt
  All unique permutations of 'exit' (24 entries), followed by every
  explicitly-supported near-miss command outside those permutations - the
  leading-"3"-for-"e" and "e_it" forms fuzzy-exit.sh also accepts (104 more
  entries, under the "# Explicit valid commands" marker).

all_4_character_combinations.txt
  Every possible lowercase 4-character string (26^4 = 456,976 entries).
  This includes every possible spelling/typo of length four, not only variants of 'exit'.
